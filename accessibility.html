Accessibility

Accessibility

Last reviewed: October 2026

Poticas.com should work for everyone — with a keyboard, with a screen reader, on a phone, at high zoom. We build toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and if any part of the site is hard to use, we want to hear about it and fix it.

What we have done

  • Semantic headings and page landmarks, so assistive technology can navigate the structure.
  • A "Skip to content" link on every page, to jump past the repeated header.
  • Text alternatives for images that carry meaning.
  • A keyboard-operable layout — every link and control is reachable without a mouse.
  • Color contrast that meets the AA threshold for body text.
  • A responsive layout that reflows on small screens and holds up at 200% zoom.

Where we know we fall short

The community page is an interactive feature that is still being refined, and some of its controls are easier to use with a mouse than without. We are working on it. The guides, recipes, history, and the makers list are the most complete, and they are where we hold the highest bar.

Tell us

If something on the site is hard to read, hard to reach, or does not work with your assistive technology, email hello@poticas.com with the page address and what went wrong. We read every message and aim to respond within a few days.

The standard we measure against

We assess the site against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. This statement is reviewed as the site changes.
